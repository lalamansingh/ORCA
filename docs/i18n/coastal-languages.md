# Coastal Indian Languages Localization Standard

## Supported Coastal Languages
1. **Hindi (`hi`)**: Northern coastal & national baseline
2. **Tamil (`ta`)**: Tamil Nadu & Puducherry coast
3. **Telugu (`te`)**: Andhra Pradesh coast
4. **Malayalam (`ml`)**: Kerala & Lakshadweep coast
5. **Gujarati (`gu`)**: Gujarat coastline & Saurashtra
6. **Marathi (`mr`)**: Maharashtra Konkan coastline
7. **Bengali (`bn`)**: West Bengal Sundarbans coastline
8. **Kannada (`kn`)**: Karnataka Karavali coast
9. **Odia (`or`)**: Odisha coastline
10. **English (`en`)**: International maritime standard
