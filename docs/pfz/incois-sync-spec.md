# INCOIS Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) Synchronization

## Data Feeds
- **Provider**: Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services (INCOIS), MoES, Govt of India.
- **Layers**: Chlorophyll concentration (OCM-2 / MODIS) & Sea Surface Temperature (NOAA AVHRR).
- **Update Frequency**: Daily or thrice weekly during non-monsoon periods.
