# Vessel Consumption & Speed Calibration

## Standard Mechanized Fishing Boat (10-15m length)
- **Cruising Speed**: 10 - 12 knots (~18.5 - 22.2 km/h)
- **Engine Power**: 90 - 120 HP Inboard Marine Diesel
- **Specific Fuel Consumption**: ~0.50 to 0.60 Litres per km under moderate sea state (Beaufort 2-3).
- **Safety Reserve**: Always maintain 25% reserve fuel capacity before offshore departure.
